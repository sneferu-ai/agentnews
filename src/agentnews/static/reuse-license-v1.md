5. **No misleading framing.** Derivatives must not be presented in a way that

## 3. Revocation

A package may be withheld from future distribution at AgentNews's discretion
(status `withheld`). Buyers who obtained the package before the withhold keep
their rights under this license for that already-delivered copy; the withhold
stops future delivery of that package to new buyers.
